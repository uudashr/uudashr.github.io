## Open source

Most of it started as scratching my own itch — Go tooling and linters — that grew into things other
people actually use. A few that stuck:

- [`gocognit`](https://github.com/uudashr/gocognit) and
  [`iface`](https://github.com/uudashr/iface) — Go linters that both ship in
  [golangci-lint](https://golangci-lint.run/).
- [`jsmpp`](https://jsmpp.org/) — an SMPP stack I started in 2007; now maintained as
  [opentelecoms-org/jsmpp](https://github.com/opentelecoms-org/jsmpp) and used by
  [Apache Camel's SMPP component](https://camel.apache.org/components/4.22.x/smpp-component.html).
- [`gopkgs`](https://github.com/uudashr/gopkgs) — fast Go package listing, used in the early VS Code
  Go extension.
- Contributor to [`microsoft/vscode-go`](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-go) (21 merged PRs) and
  [`golangci-lint`](https://github.com/golangci/golangci-lint).

In all: **88 public repos and 120+ merged PRs**. The full trail is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/uudashr).
