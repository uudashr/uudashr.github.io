Hi, I'm **Nuruddin Ashr** ([@uudashr](https://github.com/uudashr)).

I'm a software engineer and engineering leader with 21+ years building
high-scale, high-reliability systems — from telecom infrastructure in the early
2000s, through Java and Android platforms, to Go and distributed systems today.
I've worked across Indonesia, Southeast Asia, and the MENA/Gulf region in
ride-hailing, e-commerce, fintech, and media.

## The short version of my story

I spent the first half of my career going deep on craft — sockets, threading,
SMPP protocols, J2ME, Android — and the second half learning to lead. I was an
Engineering Manager at Grab (15+ engineers across ID, MY, VN, SG, CN), an
Engineering Architect at Kudo reporting directly to the CTO, and an Engineering
Manager at Kurio reporting to the CEO.

Then, in 2024, I made a deliberate move back to hands-on engineering: joining
Careem's core team migrating its ride-hailing supply-side operations onto
Uber's unified platform — systems serving 75M+ customers and 3.5M
driver-partners.

That arc is the lens for everything I write here: how good engineering and good
leadership reinforce each other — and why I think the best leaders stay close
to the code.

## What I do now

Senior Software Engineer at Careem, working on ride-hailing backend systems in
Go. Day to day: distributed systems, event-driven architecture, on-call at
scale, and shipping reliably.

## Open source

I build Go tools for code quality and developer productivity:

- [gocognit](https://github.com/uudashr/gocognit) — cognitive complexity analysis for Go
- [iface](https://github.com/uudashr/iface) — linter for interface pollution
- [gopkgs](https://github.com/uudashr/gopkgs) — Go package listing, used by the early VSCode Go plugin
- [structfield](https://github.com/uudashr/structfield) — enforces named struct literals
- [jsmpp](https://github.com/opentelecoms-org/jsmpp) — SMPP protocol stack (2007), still used by Apache Camel

I've also contributed to `golangci-lint` and `vscode-go`.
